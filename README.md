Goldshore is an original sun-soaked coastal crime game built from plain canvas modules. Explore five districts on foot or in a stolen car, dodge traffic and pedestrians, take jobs for Mara Voss's crew, and outrun the Goldshore PD while the wanted stars climb. Progress and cash are saved in the browser.

Run `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080. Move with WASD or the arrow keys, press E to steal or leave a car, aim with the mouse and click to shoot, P to pause, and M to mute.
